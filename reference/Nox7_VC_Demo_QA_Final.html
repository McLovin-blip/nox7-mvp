Scoring methodology · v2.4.1

Risk level — 0 to 25. Each risk is scored as impact (1–5) multiplied by likelihood (1–5), producing a residual score of 0–25. A threat multiplier is applied when an active advisory matches the risk profile.

Organisation level — 0 to 100. Individual risk scores are weighted by business criticality and divided by verified control coverage, producing a single 0–100 organisation posture score.

Evidence gates the score. A control with expired evidence contributes zero effectiveness — the barrier is treated as unverifiable, not as failed. This is the core rule.

AI proposes. Humans approve. Rules decide. Nox AI can propose a mapping, explain a movement, or draft a summary. It cannot alter a score.
