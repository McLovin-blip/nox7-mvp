Chrome · Top Bar & Bottom Dock Specification
Anatomy of the persistent chrome
The chrome is intentionally minimal — only the two most-used pieces of navigation. Top bar carries brand, breadcrumbs, and command. Bottom dock carries product areas. Nothing else competes.
